## Rotation rule (enforced by `voiceover-elevenlabs` skill)

- Pool size: 3-5 ElevenLabs voice IDs
- Never same voice twice in a row on this channel
- Voice IDs in this pool MUST NOT appear in any other channel's voice-pool.md (cross-channel voice bleed prevention)

## Voice selection criteria (per `brand.md` tonal rules)

- Calmly damning register — like a documentary narrator. The understatement does the heavy lifting.
- Deliberate pace with effective pauses (no rushed urgency, no shouting)
- Skeptical-curious affect, not bitter
- Trustworthy, mature register (think investigative journalist, not finance-bro hustler)
- Avoid: high-energy hype voices, anything that sounds salesy, anything cartoon-bright
- Test against a sample script with at least one specific dollar figure and one "who benefits" question — voice must land both naturally
